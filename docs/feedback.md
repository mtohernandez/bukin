Here are the issues directly from the users: 

The app logs me out every single day and making my daily punch takes forever because I have to wait for the verification token to arrive in my email.

It keeps showing location errors even when I am standing right inside the office building with high accuracy GPS turned on.

The buttons for clocking in and out do not change state or freeze after you tap them so you never know if your attendance was actually registered or not.

The facial recognition feature takes multiple attempts to capture a photo or simply crashes the entire app on the entry screen.

There is no internal panel or profile menu where a worker can verify their history of past punch-ins or check if a punch synchronized correctly.

Every time there is an update the configuration resets and I have to request the specific company login URL from my HR administrator all over again.

It works smoothly on a stable corporate Wi-Fi network but it fails completely to load the landing screen when using mobile data out in the field.

Documents like monthly work schedules or generated attendance sheets fail to render properly inside the UI or download as corrupted files.

My clock-in times get stuck locally on the device offline memory and I have to manually force a cloud sync through deep settings so I do not get marked as late.

The simple interface layout is comfortable to read and very straightforward to navigate when the login sessions do not randomly expire.

For remote office work it is highly efficient since a single click completes the registration without needing physical biometric hardware.
